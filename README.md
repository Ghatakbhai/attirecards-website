# attirecards.com – Cricket League Legends

Website for the QR codes printed on the Cricket League Legends box.

| QR code | Address | Opens |
|---|---|---|
| QR 1 | https://attirecards.com/rules | Rulebook |
| QR 2 | https://attirecards.com/score | Score tracker |

Do not rename or delete the `rules` and `score` folders or the `CNAME` file: the printed QR codes depend on them.
To update the app, replace `index.html` in all three places (root, `rules/`, `score/`) with the new version.
